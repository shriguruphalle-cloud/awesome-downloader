# Awesome Downloader 3.1.0

## What's new

- **Music, like the phone app:** four tabs (Home, Search, Your music, Social), charts on Home, the phone's cards and buttons, a Top result card in search, an artist's every song with filters, sort menus, pinned playlists, a sleep timer, recent searches, and the full-screen player's focus and lyrics views (the upright player is still there).
- **Sign in to sync** your music with the phone app, with chats, friends and listening together.
- **Browser extensions:** add any extension from the Chrome Web Store, switch it on or off, or remove it, from the new puzzle-piece button.
- **Browser tabs** open beside the page they came from, as in Chrome, and closing a tab goes to the one next to it.
- **Videos that played smeared or blocky** because of how a site encoded them are now repaired after downloading.
- **Torrents** you've finished no longer download again if you move or delete their files: they say "Moved or deleted", and you choose to download again or show where the files went.
- **A cleaner look:** one accent colour, flat buttons and calmer labels across the app.
- **Update card** at start when a new version is out.
