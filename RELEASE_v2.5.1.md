## What's new in 2.5.1

### Music
- A new Music tab: search YouTube Music, plus free music from Openverse and the
  Internet Archive, all at once, with suggestions as you type.
- Every page takes its colours from its album cover or artist photo. Artist and
  album pages, Your music (songs, artists, albums), the queue, moods.
- Songs play from a fetched copy, so they no longer skip part-way; the next song
  is fetched ahead and starts at once.
- Download any song as a tagged 320 kbps MP3 with its cover.
- Full-screen player with karaoke lyrics, in Hindi or English letters.

### Everything else
- F11 (or the new button beside the window dots) puts the whole app in full
  screen; touch the top edge to get the title bar back, Esc to leave.
- Paste anything into Video or Images: YouTube channels and Instagram accounts
  queue all their posts; links go to the right tab by themselves.
- Instagram: public accounts and every photo of a multi-photo post download
  without signing in (as far as Instagram allows); after you sign in once in
  the Browser tab, the download restarts by itself.
- Browser: bookmark star, picture-in-picture, dangerous-site blocking.
- Your history, bookmarks and queues can be encrypted on this PC (Settings).
- The installer shows FFmpeg's progress instead of seeming frozen.
